\section{DERV-02: The soldering form is $\Vhalf$-valued ---
$T_{\Eoneone}\OP=\Vhalf(\Eoneone)$}
\label{sec:derv02}

The Cayley plane $\OP=F_4/\mathrm{Spin}(9)$ is the variety of trace-one primitive
idempotents (rank-one projections) of $\hO$; it has dimension $16$ (Baez
2002, \S 3.4). Its tangent space at an idempotent $E$ is the Peirce
half-eigenspace $\Vhalf(E)$ (McCrimmon). We \emph{cite} these standard facts; the
deliverable is the exact-over-$\mathbb{Q}$ machine verification of both clauses at
$E=\Eoneone$.

\subsection{Clause (a): the Peirce half-eigenspace identity}
The left Jordan-multiplication operator $L_{\Eoneone}$ is diagonal in the
engine-native basis with eigenvalues the Peirce values $\{0,\tfrac12,1\}$,
inducing the index split
\begin{equation}
V_1(\Eoneone)=\{0\},\qquad
V_0(\Eoneone)=\{1,\dots,10\}\;(\cong\mathfrak{h}_2(\mathbb{O})),\qquad
\Vhalf(\Eoneone)=\{11,\dots,26\}\quad(\dim 16).
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[half-eigenspace identity, exact over $\mathbb{Q}$]
\label{lem:half}
For every $\delta$ in the $\Vhalf(\Eoneone)$ basis $\{b_{11},\dots,b_{26}\}$,
\begin{equation}
\Eoneone\jord\delta=\tfrac12\,\delta
\qquad\text{(exact equality over }\mathbb{Q}\text{)} .
\end{equation}
All $16$ basis elements satisfy this component-wise octonion equality.
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Clause (b): the Zariski tangent of the primitive-idempotent variety}
The rank-one locus (affine cone over $\OP$) is cut out by the single quadratic
Jordan equation $X^{\jord 2}=\Tr(X)\,X$ (Manivel, arXiv:0907.2784); with the
trace-one normalisation this is the idempotent equation
\begin{equation}
F(X):=X\jord X-X=0 .
\end{equation}
Linearising at $\Eoneone$ (the Jordan product is symmetric) gives
\begin{equation}
DF_{\Eoneone}(b)=2\,(\Eoneone\jord b)-b .
\end{equation}
We form the $27\times 27$ Jacobian $J$ whose $k$-th column is the flattened
$DF_{\Eoneone}(b_k)$ over the standard basis, and compute its kernel exactly over
$\mathbb{Q}$.

\begin{theorem}[soldering-form tangent identity]
\label{thm:tangent}
Over $\mathbb{Q}$,
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{rank} J=11=27-16,
\qquad
\dim\ker J=16,
\qquad
\ker J=\operatorname{span}\{e_{11},\dots,e_{26}\}=\Vhalf(\Eoneone) .
\end{equation}
The kernel \emph{equals} $\Vhalf(\Eoneone)$ (the index-set identity: every kernel
vector has zero component on $V_0=\{1,\dots,10\}$ and $V_1=\{0\}$), not merely a
$16$-dimensional subspace. Consequently the Zariski tangent space to the
primitive-idempotent variety at $\Eoneone$ is exactly $T_{\Eoneone}\OP=
\Vhalf(\Eoneone)$, of dimension $16$. This is consistent with the affine-cone
cross-check
\begin{equation}
16=17-1=\dim\mathrm{orbit}(\Eoneone)-1
\end{equation}
(the projective $\OP$ tangent is the affine cone over $\OP$ of dimension
$17$ minus the one scale direction; see \S\ref{sec:vald01}).
\end{theorem}

\noindent\textit{Remark (smoothness).} The bare equation $X\jord X=X$ cuts out all
idempotents (ranks $0,1,2,3$). The Zariski tangent at $\Eoneone$ is nevertheless
exactly the $16$-dimensional $\Vhalf$ because $\Eoneone$ is a smooth point of the
rank-one stratum and the strata do not meet it tangentially at first order; the
independent anchor $\dim\mathrm{orbit}(\Eoneone)=17$ certifies the projective
tangent is $17-1=16$. The optional sharp-equation cut $X^{\#}=0$ (rank $\le 1$)
is a referee-proof confirmation and is deferred (not required: the $X\jord X=X$
kernel already gives the exact $16$-dimensional $\Vhalf$).

\paragraph{Geometric consequence.} The soldering form $dE$ at $\Eoneone$ is
$\Vhalf(\Eoneone)$-valued. This is the geometric premise on which the Phase-75
coframe reduction, the Phase-76 Berry curvature, and the Phase-77 Cartan
connection stand.
