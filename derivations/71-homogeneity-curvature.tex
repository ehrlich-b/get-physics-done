\section{Route 1: the homogeneity KILL gate via cone-Hessian curvature invariants}

\subsection{The Totaro Hessian-curvature engine (3rd derivatives only)}
\label{sec:totaro-engine}

The cone metric is the Hessian of the potential $\Phi = -\log\det_3$,
\begin{equation}
  g_{ij}(X) = \partial_i\partial_j\,\Phi
            = -\,\partial_i\partial_j\,\log\det_3(X),
  \label{eq:cone-metric-71}
\end{equation}
positive-definite (Riemannian) on the cone~[FK94]. For \emph{any} Hessian metric
the metric derivative is the totally symmetric cubic form
\begin{equation}
  f_{ijk} \;:=\; g_{ij,k} \;=\; \partial_i\partial_j\partial_k\,\Phi,
  \qquad f_{ijk} = f_{(ijk)}\ \text{(totally symmetric)},
  \label{eq:cubic-form}
\end{equation}
so the first-kind Christoffel symbols collapse,
$\Gamma_{ijk} = \tfrac12(g_{ij,k}+g_{ik,j}-g_{jk,i}) = \tfrac12 f_{ijk}$, and the
Riemann tensor closes on $f$ alone. We use Totaro's closed form~[TOT04]:
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\,R_{ijkl} \;=\; -\tfrac14\, g^{pq}\big(f_{jlp}\,f_{ikq} \;-\; f_{ilp}\,f_{jkq}\big)\,}
  \label{eq:totaro}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ in ASCII the LOCKED engine token
\verb|R_ijkl = -(1/4) g^{pq}(f_jlp f_ikq - f_ilp f_jkq)|.
Because $\det_3$ is CUBIC, the engine needs only the 3rd-derivative tensor
$f_{ijk}$ -- it never forms 4th derivatives and never calls \texttt{sympy.diffgeom}
on the metric (which blows up). The inverse metric $g^{pq}$ is taken either from the
quadratic representation $P(X) = 2L(X)^2 - L(X^2)$~[McC04,FK94] or by substituting
the matter coordinates to small rationals \emph{before} inverting; the
all-symbolic \texttt{g.inv()} is never used (it times out $>200$\,s).

\paragraph{Riemann/Ricci sign convention (STATED explicitly).}
The lower-index Riemann tensor is \eqref{eq:totaro}; it is antisymmetric in
$(i,j)$ and in $(k,l)$ and symmetric under the pair swap $(ij)\leftrightarrow(kl)$
(asserted at runtime). The Ricci tensor is the $(1,3)$-contraction
$\mathrm{Ric}_{jl} = g^{ik} R_{ijkl}$, the Ricci scalar is
$R = g^{ik}g^{jl} R_{ijkl}$, the Kretschmann scalar is
$K = R_{ijkl}R^{ijkl}$ (indices raised with $g^{pq}$), and the sectional curvature
of the $2$-plane $\mathrm{span}\{u,v\}$ is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{K}(u,v) = \frac{R_{ijkl}\,u^i v^j u^k v^l}
                          {g(u,u)\,g(v,v)-g(u,v)^2}.
  \label{eq:sectional}
\end{equation}
This sign convention is pinned by the $H^3$ benchmark below (it must yield a
\emph{constant negative} sectional curvature). The decisive content for the
Phase-71 verdict is the \emph{sign} (negative $=$ hyperbolic); the magnitude is
fixed by the metric normalization (next paragraph).
