\subsection{CALC-02: second fundamental form $\mathrm{II}$ of $V_0$}

For a Hessian metric $g_{ab}=\partial_a\partial_b\Phi$ ($\Phi=-\log\det_3$), the
Levi-Civita lowered Christoffel symbol is $\Gamma_{a,bc}=\tfrac12 f_{abc}$ with
$f_{abc}=\partial_a\partial_b\partial_c\Phi$ totally symmetric (the cubic norm makes
the jet terminate). The \textbf{second fundamental form} of a submanifold with
tangent space $T$, evaluated along a $g$-normal vector $n$, is therefore
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\ \mathrm{II}^n_{bc} = g(\nabla_b\partial_c, n) = \Gamma_{a,bc}\,n^a
= \tfrac12\, f_{abc}\, n^a, \qquad b,c\in T,\ n\perp_g T.\ }
\label{eq:II}
\end{equation}
$\mathrm{II}=0$ (for all tangent $b,c$ and all $g$-normal $n$) $\iff$ the submanifold
is \emph{totally geodesic} $\iff$ $R^{\mathrm{slice}} = R^{\mathrm{ambient}}|_{\mathrm{slice}}$
(Gauss equation, ref-kollross-rodriguez-vazquez), so the slice inherits the ambient
symmetric-space curvature. Equation~\eqref{eq:II} needs no metric inverse and
addresses the \emph{full} $V_0$ tangent space.
