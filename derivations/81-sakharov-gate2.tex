\section{A scalar cannot change tensor support}\label{sec:scalar-inv}

\begin{lemma}[Scalar invariance of support]\label{lem:scalar-inv}
For any nonzero scalar $\kappa$ and any tensor $T$, $\supp(\kappa T)=\supp(T)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$(\kappa T)_{\mu\nu}=\kappa\,T_{\mu\nu}$; since $\kappa\neq0$, $(\kappa T)_{\mu\nu}\neq0
\iff T_{\mu\nu}\neq0$. \end{proof}

Computed (not asserted) over $\QQ$: for the induced $\kappa=6\pi^2/\Lambda_f^2$ of
\eqref{eq:induced-kappa}, for a symbolic free scalar $\kappa$, and for a fixed-seed rational
panel of eight nonzero scalars, $\supp(\kappa T^{[\psi]})=\supp(T^{[\psi]})$ with $n=6$ in every
case. In particular the induced scalar's effect on the support is \emph{independent of
$\Lambda_f$}---which is the whole point: no value of the Gate-3 scale can change which
components the matter stress occupies.

\paragraph{Why naive support coverage is not the obstruction.} The metric $g=\eu\!\cdot\!\eu$
is \emph{dense}: at the anchor all $16$ entries are nonzero, so
\begin{equation}
\supp(\kappa T+\Lambda g)=\supp(T)\cup\supp(g)=\{00,\dots,33\}\quad(\text{all }16).
\end{equation}
Adding the cosmological term $\Lambda g$ \emph{does} reach every component as a support set. The
obstruction is therefore not that $\kappa T+\Lambda g$ fails to cover $\supp(G)$; it is that it
cannot \emph{equal} $G$, which the next section makes precise.

\section{The decisive off-$T$ over-determination}\label{sec:offT}

\begin{proposition}[Off-$T$ block]\label{prop:offT}
Let $\mathcal{O}=\supp(G)\setminus\supp(T)$ be the entries where $G\neq0$ but $T=0$. On
$\mathcal{O}$ the Einstein equation $G_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}$ reduces to
$G_{\mu\nu}=\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}$, independently of $\kappa$. A single scalar $\Lambda$ solves
this iff the ratios $\{G_{\mu\nu}/g_{\mu\nu}:(\mu,\nu)\in\mathcal{O}\}$ are all equal.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For $(\mu,\nu)\in\mathcal{O}$, $T_{\mu\nu}=0$, so $\kappa T_{\mu\nu}=0$ for any scalar
$\kappa$; the equation is $G_{\mu\nu}=\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}$. Since $g_{\mu\nu}\neq0$ there
(g is dense), $\Lambda=G_{\mu\nu}/g_{\mu\nu}$; consistency across $\mathcal{O}$ requires a single
value. \end{proof}

Exactly over $\QQ$ at the anchor, $\mathcal{O}$ has $\mathbf{10}$ entries
($\{00,02,03,11,12,13,20,21,30,31\}$), and the required ratios $G_{\mu\nu}/g_{\mu\nu}$ take
$\mathbf{4}$ \emph{distinct} values---so \emph{no single scalar $\Lambda$ works}, and $\kappa$
has dropped out entirely. The matter coupling is irrelevant on the off-$T$ block; the failure is
purely a property of $G$ versus $g$. This is the precise sense in which a scalar coupling cannot
repair a tensor-rank mismatch.
