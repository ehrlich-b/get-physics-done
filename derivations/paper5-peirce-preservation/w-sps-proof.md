## Section 1 — Statement

**Theorem (W carries the product-form sequential product in sense (b)).**
Let `V_B`, `V_M` be finite-dim archimedean order unit spaces over ℝ, each
equipped with a sequential product `∘_{V_B}`, `∘_{V_M}` satisfying the vdW 2019
Definition 2 axioms (S1-S7). Let `V_{BM}` be the composite OUS per Paper 5
composite-lt.tex §composite-def (frozen line range: L18-40 in the living file),
and let

```
W := span_ℝ { a_i ⊗ b_j : a_i ∈ basis(V_B), b_j ∈ basis(V_M) }  ⊆  V_{BM}
```

be the algebraic-tensor-product subspace. Equip W with:

- **Induced order** `≤|_W` := restriction of `≤_{V_{BM}}` to W,
- **Order unit** `1_W := 1_{V_{BM}} = 1_B ⊗ 1_M ∈ W`,
- **Induced positive cone** `W^+ := W ∩ V_{BM}^+`,
- **Restricted sequential product** `∘|_W := ∘_{V_{BM}} |_{[0,1]_W × [0,1]_W}`
  (set-theoretic restriction; not a new operation).

Then `(W, ≤|_W, 1_W, ∘|_W)` satisfies vdW 2019 Definition 2 axioms S1-S7 — i.e.,
**sense (b) of "carries" per `carries-senses.md §2`** holds on W.

**Consequences.**
1. By vdW 2019 Theorem 1 applied to W, the finite-dim SPS W is order-isomorphic
   to a Euclidean Jordan algebra (stronger than needed for the `sms:minimal`
   upper-bound use; recorded for downstream benefit).
2. By `ci-sps-morphism.md` §6 (sibling Plan 56-02 Task 3 artifact), **sense
   (c)** (ι : W ↪ V_{BM} is an SPS-morphism) follows as a free corollary,
   because `1_W = 1_{V_{BM}}` and `∘|_W` is a set-theoretic restriction.

**Downstream identity context.** This theorem is the content of the upper-bound
step of `thm:local-tomo` (composite-lt.tex:203-221) and `thm:lt-full`
(appendix-proofs.tex:228-238), as extracted verbatim in
`.gpd/phases/56-thm-5-8-upper-bound-w-carries-product-form-sequential-product/thm-5-8-identity-verbatim.md`
Sections 2-3. Plan 56-03 will integrate the revised proof text into these sites.

## Section 2 — PRIMARY ROUTE (vdW 2019 Def. 4 + Thm 1)

**Step 1 — W coincides with the algebraic tensor product.**

`W = span_ℝ{a_i ⊗ b_j}` is, by definition of the algebraic tensor product,
equal to the image of the canonical bilinear map
`V_B × V_M → V_{BM}, (a, b) ↦ a ⊗ b`. Equivalently, `W = V_B ⊗_ℝ V_M` as a
real-linear subspace of `V_{BM}`. The order unit `1_B ⊗ 1_M` lies in W because
`1_B ∈ span(a_i)` (since `{a_i}` is a basis of V_B, which includes/generates
the unit) and similarly `1_M ∈ span(b_j)`.

**Step 2 — Invoke vdW 2019 Definition 4 (locally tomographic composite).**

vdW 2019 Definition 4 introduces the notion of a **locally tomographic
composite** of two sequential-product spaces. In the verbatim formulation of
vdW 2019 §I (see the arXiv:1803.11139 preprint for the definition text), the
composite of two SPS's `(V_1, ∘_1)`, `(V_2, ∘_2)` is **locally tomographic**
when it equals `V_1 ⊗_ℝ V_2` (as ordered vector spaces, with the projective
cone generated by product positives) AND the product-form sequential product

```
(a ⊗ b) ∘ (c ⊗ d) := (a ∘_1 c) ⊗ (b ∘_2 d)                       (*)
```

satisfies vdW 2019 Definition 2 (S1-S7). Equivalently, Definition 4 is a
**structure-preservation theorem**: if (V_1, ∘_1) and (V_2, ∘_2) are SPS, then
`V_1 ⊗_ℝ V_2` equipped with (*) is also an SPS.

In our setting:

- **V_B and V_M are SPSs.** The submitted version of Paper 5, §4, establishes
  `prop:inheritance` type results showing S1-S7 hold on V_B and on V_M
  separately. (This is the factor-level SPS structure; Phases 54-55 have
  audited the factor-level proofs and certified A-S Ch. ≤ 8 compliance.)
- **(W, ∘|_W) matches Definition 4 VERBATIM.** The product-form identity
  `\seqp{(a ⊗ b)}{(c ⊗ d)} = (\seqp{a}{c}) ⊗ (\seqp{b}{d})` is the Paper 5
  defining equation of the product-form SP on `W` (composite-lt.tex:215-217;
  appendix-proofs.tex:232-233). The set-theoretic restriction ∘|_W is
  literally (*) because every element of `[0,1]_W` has a product-effect
  representation by the basis expansion, and ∘|_W on product effects is
  given by the product-form identity. On non-product-effect elements of
  [0,1]_W, the identity extends by bilinearity.

**Conclusion of Step 2:** By vdW 2019 Definition 4, `(W, ∘|_W)` is a finite-dim
sequential product space satisfying vdW 2019 Definition 2 (S1-S7). Sense (b)
of carries-senses.md §2 is ESTABLISHED.

**Step 3 — Invoke vdW 2019 Theorem 1 (closeout).**

vdW 2019 Theorem 1 states that every finite-dim SPS (i.e., every structure
satisfying vdW Def. 2 in finite dimension) is order-isomorphic to a Euclidean
Jordan algebra. Applying Theorem 1 to W: **W is order-isomorphic to an EJA.**

This stronger conclusion is not strictly needed for the `sms:minimal`
upper-bound application (which requires only that W is an OUS satisfying
(C1)-(C4) and the product-form SP, i.e., sense (b)), but it is free and
feeds sense (c) via BGW 2020 monoidal-category framing (see
`ci-sps-morphism.md` and `carries-three-sense-table.md` Section 2). ∎

**Length check.** Section 2 is ≤ 2 pages as the primary-route budget requires.

**Anchor note on Def. 4 not being circular.** Definition 4 is an
algebra-structure definition: it names the condition under which a composite
is "locally tomographic" and states that the product-form SP makes the tensor
into an SPS. Invoking Def. 4 here is not circular because we are using the
**structural content** of the definition (the composite is an SPS), grounded
on the hypothesis that V_B and V_M are SPSs at the factor level. The
factor-level SPS hypothesis is independently established by Paper 5 §4 (not
by Phase 56).

## Section 4 — Conclusion

By Section 2 (primary route) or Section 3 (fallback route), `(W, ∘|_W)`
satisfies vdW 2019 Definition 2 (S1-S7) and is therefore a finite-dim
sequential product space. By vdW 2019 Theorem 1 applied to W, W is
order-isomorphic to a Euclidean Jordan algebra.

**Sense established.** Sense (b) per `carries-senses.md §2`:
`(W, ≤|_W, 1_W, ∘|_W)` is an SPS in the vdW 2019 Def. 2 sense.
Sense (c) (ι : W ↪ V_{BM} is an SPS-morphism) follows immediately as a free
corollary — proven in the companion artifact `ci-sps-morphism.md` — because
`1_W = 1_{V_{BM}}` (the order unit of W coincides with that of V_{BM}) and
`∘|_W` is the set-theoretic restriction of `∘_{V_{BM}}` (no alternative
W-internal operation is introduced).

`thm:local-tomo` (composite-lt.tex:203-221) and `thm:lt-full`
(appendix-proofs.tex:228-238). It licenses the minimality argument
