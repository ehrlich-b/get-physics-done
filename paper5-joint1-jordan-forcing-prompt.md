**Type:** prove-or-disprove a single specific claim (with a decision tree).
**Supersedes:** `paper5-recognition-fit-prompt.md` (dead — it asked "does Def-1 entail

## 0. The one claim to settle

> **CLAIM J1.** Definition 1 (the four self-modeling clauses) together with the corrected
> sequential-product construction eq:sp-explicit **forces** Euclidean Jordan structure.
> Equivalently: **there is no finite-dimensional, non-Jordan, spectral order-unit space that
> (a) satisfies all four clauses of Definition 1 and (b) supports the construction of
> eq:sp-explicit.**

Prove it or disprove it. The disproof is a single explicit model; the proof is a principled
exclusion of the candidate models plus one missing lemma. Both outcomes are publishable and
both are wanted — this decides whether Paper 5 has a *forcing* step or is a labeled synthesis.

## 1. Why this is THE joint (context — read once)

The spine of Paper 5 is:

```
self-modeling (Def 1)
  → the loop builds the corrected sequential product  eq:sp-explicit
  → that product satisfies sequential-product axioms S1–S7
  → [van de Wetering 2018, Thm 1, BICONDITIONAL]  the space is a Euclidean Jordan algebra
  → [self-duality / S4]  ...  → [local tomography / central-i]  complex M_n(C)
```

van de Wetering's theorem is a **biconditional**: a space carries a sequential product
satisfying S1–S7 **iff** it is a Euclidean Jordan algebra (EJA). So a non-Jordan space carries
**no** valid S1–S7 product at all. That has a sharp consequence:

- If some non-Jordan space **V** satisfies Def-1 and supports eq:sp-explicit, then on **V** the
  corrected product **cannot** satisfy S1–S7 (vdW). Hence Def-1 does **not** entail "the
  corrected product satisfies S1–S7" — the paper's axiom-verification (sections/axiom-
  verification.tex) must be covertly using a property **V** lacks. CLAIM J1 is **FALSE**:
  self-modeling does **not** force Jordan; self-duality (S4) is a genuine independent import.
- If, instead, **every** non-Jordan candidate fails Def-1 or cannot host the construction
  (and the missing proof step §3.T1 closes), then CLAIM J1 is **TRUE**: Jordan is forced.

This is the exact analogue of the complex step. There, the rebit M_n(R) ⊨ (field-blind core)
but isn't complex, proving the complex selector (central-i / local tomography) is an import.
**The open question is whether the *same* independence holds one level up — at the Jordan
step.** Two non-Jordan witnesses have been *asserted* to satisfy Def-1 but have **not been
verified**. Verifying them is the whole task.

## 2. The objects (self-contained definitions)

### 2.1 The corrected sequential product (eq:sp-explicit)

For an effect/observable `a` with spectral decomposition `a = Σ_i λ_i p_i` (orthogonal
idempotents `p_i`, eigenvalues `λ_i ∈ [0,1]`):

```
   ⟪a,b⟫  =  Σ_i λ_i · C_{p_i}(b)   +   Σ_{i<j} √(λ_i λ_j) · P_{ij}(b)
```

- `C_{p}` = the Alfsen–Shultz **compression** onto the face generated by idempotent `p`
  (a positive linear map; AS 2003, Ch. 7).
- `P_{ij}` = the **Peirce (i,j) projection** onto the 1-space `V_1(p_i, p_j)`
  (a linear combination of compressions).

This is the order-theoretic Peirce expansion of the Lüders map `√a · b · √a`. It is well-defined
**only** on a space that actually has spectral decompositions, compressions, and Peirce
projections — i.e. a spectral order-unit space with a compression system. **Whether the
candidate witnesses below genuinely have this structure is part of the question** (do not assume
it; construct `C_p` and `P_ij` explicitly on each candidate, or show they cannot exist).

### 2.2 Definition 1 (the four self-modeling clauses — read main.tex:357–376 for exact wording)

1. **(spectral OUS)** finite-dimensional spectral order-unit space — every effect has a
   spectral resolution `a = Σ λ_i p_i` into orthogonal idempotents.
2. **(faithful φ)** a faithful self-modeling map φ (in the witnesses below take φ = id).
3. **(minimal composite)** the minimal composite OUS carrying product states, product effects,
   non-signaling, equipped with the product-form sequential product.
4. **(simple)** simplicity / irreducibility.

**KNOWN HAZARD (do not paper over — report it):** clause (3) is read two ways in the manuscript.
In the separation/independence arguments it is weak ("*a* minimal composite exists") so the
rebit passes; in type-exclusion.tex (lines ~55–67) it is strong ("minimality ⟹ local
tomography") so the rebit is *excluded* via 9 ≠ 10. The same equivocation governs the Jordan
step. **For THIS task, use clause (3) in the weak, honest form** (a minimal composite exists),
and flag explicitly wherever the strong reading would change a verdict. If a witness satisfies
clauses (1),(2),(4) and the weak (3) but fails the strong (3), say so precisely — that *is* the
finding (it shows clause (3) is doing the importing).

### 2.3 The sequential-product axioms S1–S7

Use van de Wetering 2018 (arXiv:1803.11139) Def./Thm 1 verbatim. The ones in play here:
- **S4** (symmetry of orthogonality / the self-duality-linked axiom).
- **S2** (continuity), **S5–S7** (the Peirce/compatibility axioms).
S1 (additivity) and S3 (the positivity/unit axioms) are not in dispute.

## 3. The two targets

### T1 — the lattice-compatibility bridge (the load-bearing Jordan-side proof step)

In sections/axiom-verification.tex (~l.236–254, Lemma `peirce-vanish` → S5–S7), the proof needs:

> `a · b = b · a` for the **corrected product** ⟹ the spectral projectors of `a` and `b` are
> **lattice-compatible** (`C_{p_i} ∘ C_{q_j} = C_{q_j} ∘ C_{p_i}`).

This is currently justified only by citing Alfsen–Shultz Prop 7.49 + Niestegge 2009 Lemma 3.3,
which are about compatibility under the **lattice/compression** relation — **not** under
commutation of *this* product. In M_n it is trivial because all three relations coincide, which
is exactly the conclusion.

**T1 task:** Either
- **(prove)** derive, *from eq:sp-explicit directly and without assuming Jordan structure*, that
  `⟪a,b⟫ = ⟪b,a⟫` ⟹ lattice-compatibility of the spectral projectors (equivalently
  `P_1^{(a)}(b) = 0`); **or**
- **(disprove)** exhibit a spectral OUS with a compression system where `⟪a,b⟫ = ⟪b,a⟫` holds
  but the projectors are **not** lattice-compatible.

Also resolve the S2 sub-gap (axiom-verification.tex ~l.54–71): the proof shows the *scalar*
coefficients merge at degeneracies (`√(λ_iλ_j) → λ_i` as `λ_j → λ_i`) but `C_{p_i}, P_{ij}`
**jump** there; show the operator-valued map `a ↦ ⟪a,·⟫` is norm-continuous through degeneracies
(projector jumps cancel the coefficient merge), or show it is not.

### T2 — the witness verification (the decider for CLAIM J1)

Two non-Jordan candidates have been *asserted* (Paper 5 deep-note §11) to satisfy Def-1. **Verify
or refute each, explicitly and by construction.** This is the heart of the task.

**(a) Niestegge-2024 rank-2 generalized qubit** (arXiv:2208.07135) — proposed as the
**in-framework** witness (it is claimed to bear compressions yet be non-self-dual).
- Does it satisfy Def-1 clauses (1),(2),(4) + weak (3)?
- Can `C_p`, `P_ij`, and hence eq:sp-explicit be **constructed** on it explicitly?
- Is it genuinely **non-Jordan** (non-self-dual), or does rank-2 degeneracy collapse it to a
  Jordan ball? (AS 7.49/7.50 are near-vacuous with a single atom pair — check this is not
  hiding the answer.)
- VERDICT: `Niestegge ⊨ Def-1 + supports construction + non-Jordan` = TRUE or FALSE.

**(b) Vinberg T-algebra** (Vinberg 1960/63; vdW §VII) — the canonical homogeneous, non-self-dual,
non-Jordan cone.
- §11 flags it as possibly **off-framework** ("not a full AS spectral OUS"). Settle it: does
  Vinberg have spectral decompositions, compressions `C_p`, and Peirce projections `P_ij`
  sufficient to *define* eq:sp-explicit? Construct them or prove they cannot exist.
- If the construction **cannot** be built on Vinberg, its exclusion is **principled** (it is not
  a self-modeling system), not special-pleading — state exactly which structural ingredient is
  missing (facial absorption AS 7.43? transitivity of the compression lattice?).
- If the construction **can** be built, Vinberg ⊨ Def-1 and CLAIM J1 is FALSE.
- VERDICT: `Vinberg ⊨ Def-1 + supports construction` = TRUE or FALSE.

**Framework-honesty check (Agent-1 concern):** the spine cites Barnum–Hilgert (arXiv:1904.03753,
Thm 1.1: "spectral AND strongly symmetric ⟺ Jordan") for "spectral ⊊ Jordan." But the
construction uses the **full AS compression calculus including facial absorption 7.43**. Does
Barnum–Hilgert's "spectral" entail 7.43? If the construction's "spectral OUS" already entails
7.43 + the full calculus, is that strictly weaker than Jordan, or does it covertly re-impose it?
Exhibit a **rank ≥ 3** non-Jordan model of {7.23, 7.43, 7.49, 7.50 + spectral decomposition}, or
prove none exists. (Rank-2 is too degenerate to settle this — that is why T2(a) needs the
rank/degeneracy check.)

## 5. Guards (anti-glaze / anti-thrash)

- **Do NOT assume Jordan structure** anywhere in T1 or in constructing `C_p`/`P_ij` for T2.
  The whole point is whether the construction needs it. If a step is "trivial in M_n," that is a
  red flag, not a proof — it means the M_n coincidence is doing the work.
- **Verify, do not cite.** §11 *asserted* the Niestegge and Vinberg witnesses; this task is to
  CHECK them by explicit construction. "It is known that..." is not acceptable for the witness
  verdicts.
- **Exact arithmetic** (ℚ / ℤ / algebraic numbers) for any concrete small model; no floating
  point for a yes/no structural verdict.
- **Report the clause-(3) equivocation honestly.** If a witness passes under weak (3) and fails
  under strong (3)=LT, that is a *finding about where the import enters*, not a failure to
  decide — record it precisely.
- **Distinguish DEFLATION from SURRENDER.** "CLAIM J1 is false" is a real, defensible result IF
  backed by a verified model; it is NOT a license to wave the joint away. Conversely, do not
  declare J1 TRUE on a candidate-specific exclusion dressed up as generic — name the residual
  class if your exclusion does not obviously cover all non-Jordan construction-supporting spaces.
