## Background (why this is the math half of the hard problem)

The target is Mary's / Chalmers' gap: a complete third-person description does not
determine the what-it-is-like. The rho/Phi split makes it precise:

- **rho_J(X) = det(X) * (Tr(X^2) - 1/3)** is a polynomial in the single-state
  F_4-invariants (Tr, Tr^2, det). THIRD-PERSON correlate: Observable, computable by anyone.
  (THEOREM: rho_J is the unique minimal F_4-invariant with the stated boundary conditions.)
  The complete third-person record of a Stream is the rho-trajectory.
- **Phi[Stream] = sum_k rho_J(X_k) * ||X_{k+1} - X_k||_J^2** is a functional on the whole
  trajectory. Expanding the norm, its load-bearing piece is the CROSS-TERM
  c(X_k, X_{k+1}) = Tr(X_k o X_{k+1}), coupling consecutive frames. Candidate FIRST-PERSON
  magnitude.

The Chalmers gap, in this language: **the rho-trajectory does not determine Phi** - because
Phi needs the cross-term, and the cross-term is not a function of the single-state
invariants. "Give me every correlate down to the quantum level [all of rho], I still won't
know what it is like [Phi]" becomes the precise statement that the cross-term is independent
of the invariant ring. THIS MILESTONE PROVES THAT (the (RING) claim below).

The other half of the gap - why a third party gets only the invariants and not the
cross-term - is the FRAME-QUOTIENT: the cross-term needs the relative gauge between
consecutive states; a third party not sharing the subject's frame is restricted to
gauge-invariants (QRF / no-broadcasting). That is a framework-level identification, NOT part
of this milestone; this milestone is the invariant-theory half.

(Correction 2026-05-24: an earlier framing aimed at SELF-inaccessibility - "the system can't
measure its OWN Phi," via reducible/irreducible self-modeling dynamics. That is a STRONGER,

## Inline definitions (authoritative)

- **h_3(O):** 3x3 Hermitian octonionic matrices (the Albert algebra), 27-dim real, with
  Jordan product X o Y = (1/2)(XY + YX). Formally real, simple, the UNIQUE non-special
  simple formally real Jordan algebra.
- **F_4 = Aut(h_3(O)):** 52-dim compact exceptional Lie group; acts on the trace-free
  **26** irreducibly, fixes the identity. Preserves Tr, the trace form, and the cubic norm.
- **Tr(X):** Jordan trace (sum of the 3 real diagonal entries). **Tr(X^2)** via the trace
  form. **det(X):** the cubic norm N(X) (Freudenthal/Springer cubic form); det and Tr^3,
  Tr*Tr^2 span the degree-3 invariants.
- **Single-state invariant ring:** R[h_3(O)]^{F_4} = R[Tr, Tr^2, det] (Faraut-Koranyi,
  *Analysis on Symmetric Cones*, Ch. V; Springer). Three algebraically independent
  generators. THIS IS THE "OBSERVABLE" RING.
- **Cross-term:** c(X,Y) = Tr(X o Y), the Jordan trace-form pairing of two states. A
  diagonal-F_4 invariant on the PAIR.
- **rho_J, Phi:** as in Background.
