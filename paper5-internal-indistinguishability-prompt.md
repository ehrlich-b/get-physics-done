## Definitions (fix these FIRST; the whole GPD lives or dies on non-circularity here)

**Internal phenomenology** of a Def-1 self-modeler = the totality of operational facts accessible through
its own loop: statistics of its readable (product/record) sector, which of its own dynamical quantities
admit records, and the update behavior of its own records — NOTHING that requires an external observer's
tomography of the composite. The rename trap: "like us" must NOT be defined as "is complex" or via any
quantity only an outside observer can measure. The seam: operational only — no ρ/Φ, no qualia.

## CLAIM II-a (internal detectability)

The genuine M_n(ℝ) self-modeler's two structural deficits are detectable in its OWN internal
phenomenology:
- **D1 (energy-recordability):** its reversible generators are so(n), not observables (⟦a,b⟧ ∉ V — repo
  §13, exact). Candidate internal experiment: the modeler tomographs its own dynamics through its loop
  (measure, evolve, measure — all internal), reconstructs the generator as a map, and checks it against
  its own observable set; finds no observable generates its flow (no internal spectroscopy, no energy
  records). Decide: is this experiment actually performable with product/record effects only, and does
  its outcome differ from the M_n(ℂ) case? Or does a process-tomography surrogate give the ℝ modeler
  everything "energy" gives us, from inside?
- **D2 (self-history transparency):** the real self-composite has joint-state dimensions its product
  records cannot resolve (9<10 at n=2; readable 3^k vs composite growth — repo §12/§19, exact). Decide:
  does the unreadable sector couple back into readable statistics under the loop dynamics (from inside:
  irreducible excess unpredictability — two internally-identical record-states with different readable
  futures)? Construct the explicit 2-rebit instance or prove the coupling vanishes (the latter
  half-builds the counter-witness).

## CLAIM II-b (masquerade collapse)

Any Def-1 self-modeler whose internal phenomenology matches a ℂ self-modeler's IS order-isomorphic to
one. Target to collapse: real-vector-space QM with a universal rebit/J-ancilla, which reproduces all
complex statistics (Aleksandrova–Borish–Wootters arXiv:1210.4535; McKague–Mosca–Gisin quant-ph/0810.1923).
The two moves to formalize: (1) the simulation's operational effect algebra (what its internal

ancilla J is a distinguished global structure shared across all subsystems — a preferred structure, i.e.
a B-violation — and it breaks Def-1(iii) independence (the "model" tick cannot be non-signaling-separated
from the ancilla). Prove (1) and (2) at Def-1 grade or exhibit the gap.
