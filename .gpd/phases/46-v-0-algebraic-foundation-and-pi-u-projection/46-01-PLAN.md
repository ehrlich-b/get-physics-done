    1. **Implement jordan_product_h2o(A, B) -> H3O**: The intrinsic Jordan product on h_2(O), treating it as a 2x2 Hermitian octonionic matrix algebra.

       For A = (beta_A, gamma_A, x1_A) and B = (beta_B, gamma_B, x1_B), corresponding to 2x2 matrices:
         A_mat = [[beta_A, conj(x1_A)], [x1_A, gamma_A]]
         B_mat = [[beta_B, conj(x1_B)], [x1_B, gamma_B]]

       The matrix product AB has entries:
         (AB)_{11} = beta_A * beta_B + conj(x1_A) * x1_B
         (AB)_{12} = beta_A * conj(x1_B) + conj(x1_A) * gamma_B
         (AB)_{21} = x1_A * beta_B + gamma_A * x1_B
         (AB)_{22} = x1_A * conj(x1_B) + gamma_A * gamma_B

       Note: conj(x1_A) * x1_B gives a scalar Re part + vector Im part. The scalar part is the real inner product <x1_A, x1_B> = sum of component products.

       Jordan product = (1/2)(AB + BA), extract the Hermitian (beta, gamma, x1) entries.

       The diagonal entries (11) and (22) are automatically real (sum of real scalar products).
       The off-diagonal entry is (1/2)((AB)_{21} + (BA)_{21}).

       CRITICAL: This is the INTRINSIC h_2(O) product, NOT the inherited h_3(O) product. The h_3(O) product embeds the 2x2 block in a 3x3 matrix and may produce V_{1/2} leakage. The intrinsic product works directly on 2x2 matrices and closes by definition.
