## SUSPECT / NEEDS-RERUN detail

There are **no SUSPECT or NEEDS-RERUN items inside the two requested papers** (Paper 7
and the v15.0 result). Every decisive claim in both stays in the safe zone
(diagonal/real/complex-slice, Spin(9) realization, representation-theoretic eigenvalues,
classification, or PSD-yes/no / det-free residual).

The one NEEDS-RERUN is the **related but out-of-scope** `h_3_O-measure-2026.tex`:

- **What is at risk:** the paper's central object is the F_4-invariant Stream measure
  Φ[Stream] = Σ_k ρ_J(X_k) ‖X_{k+1}−X_k‖² with ρ_J(X) = det(X)·(Tr(X²)−1/3). It treats
  `det X` as one of the three Faraut–Korányi generators of R[h_3(O)]^{F_4} and builds the
  whole Observable/Beable split, the ρ_J uniqueness theorem (thm:rho-uniqueness), and the
  Φ-family enumeration on top of it. If `det` is computed with the buggy cross term, then
  (i) ρ_J is not actually F_4-invariant on octonionic states, (ii) "Observable = polynomial
  in {Tr, Tr², det}" mis-identifies the invariant ring, and (iii) the vanishing locus of ρ_J
  (rank-deficient ∂Ω) is the det=0 hypersurface, which moves under the correction.
- **What to re-check:** whether the paper's det is only ever evaluated symbolically /
  abstractly (in which case the LaTeX is fine and only any supporting numeric code needs the
  fix) or whether any numerical instantiation used the buggy `octonion_algebra.det_3`. The
  algebraic claims (F_4-invariance of det, vanishing on rank-deficient stratum) are TRUE for
  the correct norm, so the paper's prose is likely salvageable; any numerics that fed
  specific octonionic det/ρ_J values need re-running. This should be triaged separately.
