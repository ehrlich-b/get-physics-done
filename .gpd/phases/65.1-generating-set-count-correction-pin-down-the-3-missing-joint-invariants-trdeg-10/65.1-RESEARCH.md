## Summary

This is a NARROW corrective phase. Phase 65 COMPUTED (triple-confirmed) that the generic pair orbit dimension of F_4 on h_3(O) ⊕ h_3(O) (diagonal action) is **44**, so trdeg R[27⊕27]^{F_4} = 54 − 44 = **10**, not the naive-anchor 7. The "six pointwise + c" set is therefore functionally INCOMPLETE by exactly **3** joint invariants. trdeg 10 = 3(X) + 3(Y) + **4 mixed** (c plus 3 more). This phase pins down the 3 missing functionally-independent mixed invariants by **confirming a 10-candidate set realizes exact Jacobian rank 10 over Q** at ≥3 generic rational points, and — if the natural candidates fall short — by walking a deterministic fallback ladder to a minimal completing set. COMPUTE, do not assume.

The method is ALREADY LOCKED (Derksen-Kemper char-0, dual statement): for explicit invariants f_1..f_10 built as SymPy polynomials in the 54 symbols, the transcendence degree of the subfield they generate equals the generic rank over Q of their 10×54 Jacobian [∂f_i/∂z_j]. If that rank == 10 == the Phase-65 orbit-derived trdeg, the candidates are **functionally complete at the field level** (they capture every functional degree of freedom; the full invariant field is algebraic over them). **This does NOT prove they generate the RING** — that is Phase 68's Hilbert-series/Krull job. This phase establishes field/trdeg-level completeness only; the RESEARCH is explicit about that boundary everywhere.

The candidate family is strongly literature-supported: Iltyakov (1995) + Polikarpov (1991) established the **Artin–Procesi–Iltyakov equality** for the split Albert algebra in char 0 for m ∈ {1,2} elements — the invariant ring of a PAIR EQUALS the **trace algebra** (generated by traces of Jordan-product monomials). So the missing generators are trace monomials, and the natural completing candidates are exactly the mixed-bidegree trace monomials Tr(X²∘Y) (2,1), Tr(X∘Y²) (1,2), Tr(X²∘Y²) (2,2). An in-engine check (this RESEARCH) further shows these trace monomials have **no grouping ambiguity** (trace associativity makes Tr((X∘X)∘Y) = Tr(X∘(X∘Y))), so the executor cannot introduce an ordering bug.

**Primary recommendation:** Build the 10 candidate invariants as SymPy polynomials in the 54 engine symbols (reuse `inv_*` for the 7 base; add `Tr(jordan(jordan(Xsym,Xsym),Ysym))`, `Tr(jordan(Xsym,jordan(Ysym,Ysym)))`, `Tr(jordan(jordan(Xsym,Xsym),jordan(Ysym,Ysym)))` for the 3 mixed). Take the 54 symbolic partials of each (sympy `diff`), substitute a generic INTEGER point FIRST (per `_gradients_in_X`), stack into a 10×54 rational matrix, take `exact_qq_rank` (DomainMatrix-over-QQ) at ≥3 generic integer points, MAX. Assert MAX == 10. Compute the fine-grained tier increments (6→7→8→9→10) so the redundant candidate (if any) is pinpointed. If rank < 10, walk the deterministic fallback ladder (Section "Alternative Approaches") until a minimal completing set of trdeg 10 is found — COMPUTE, do not assume.

## Standard Approaches

### Approach 1: Candidate-Jacobian rank over Q (RECOMMENDED — the locked method)

**What:** Confirm the 10-candidate set `{Tr X, Tr X², det X, Tr Y, Tr Y², det Y, c, Tr(X²∘Y), Tr(X∘Y²), Tr(X²∘Y²)}` has exact Jacobian rank 10 over Q at ≥3 generic integer points (MAX), matching the Phase-65 orbit-derived trdeg 10.

**Why standard / why correct:** Derksen-Kemper §4 char-0 Jacobian criterion — the generic rank of the Jacobian of f_1..f_k equals the transcendence degree of the field they generate. Rank 10 == orbit-derived trdeg 10 means the 10 candidates are algebraically independent and the invariant field R(27⊕27)^{F_4} is algebraic over Q(f_1..f_10), i.e. **field-level functionally complete**. This is the EXACT DUAL of the Phase-65 orbit-dimension computation (same engine, same exact-rank recipe), so the machinery is certified.

**Track record:** The orbit-dim half of this exact pair (rank of the 52×54 infinitesimal-action matrix = 44) was triple-confirmed in Phase 65 with the identical `exact_qq_rank` recipe. The single-copy 24 (and hence single-copy trdeg 3) was reproduced. The Jacobian-of-invariants half reuses the same substitute-first / DomainMatrix machinery on a 10×54 (vs 52×54) matrix — strictly cheaper.

**Key steps (executor recipe):**
1. Reuse the 7 base invariants `inv_Tr_X, inv_Tr2_X, inv_det_X, inv_Tr_Y, inv_Tr2_Y, inv_det_Y, inv_c` from `ring_lemma_verification.py` (already SymPy exprs in the 54 symbols).
2. Build the 3 mixed: `f_21 = Tr(jordan(jordan(Xsym,Xsym), Ysym))`, `f_12 = Tr(jordan(Xsym, jordan(Ysym,Ysym)))`, `f_22 = Tr(jordan(jordan(Xsym,Xsym), jordan(Ysym,Ysym)))`. (These compose without error — engine-checked, this RESEARCH.)
3. For each f_i, compute the 54 partials `[diff(f_i, z) for z in xs+ys]`. (Symbolic gradient; det X is deg 3 and f_22 is deg 4 — see expression-swell control below.)
4. At each generic integer pair point (reuse `PAIR_POINTS`): substitute the point into the 54 partials FIRST (rationals), assemble the 10×54 rational matrix, take `exact_qq_rank`.
5. Take MAX over ≥3 generic integer points. ASSERT MAX == 10.
6. Cross-check the decisive rank with `exact_rank_route_crosscheck` (three exact domains agree) and the `exact_only_guard` (0 numpy float-rank calls).

**Known difficulties at each step:**
- Step 3 (gradient of det X / f_22): expression swell. The det-X gradient is already handled in `_gradients_in_X` (heavy but only in the 27 X-symbols). f_22 (Tr(X²∘Y²), deg 4) is the heaviest new gradient (it has both X- and Y-content). Mitigation: substitute the integer point into the partials FIRST (do NOT `simplify()` the symbolic gradient); keep tangents as matrix·vector where possible; use SMALL integer coordinates to avoid denominator blowup. If the symbolic `diff` of f_22 is slow, build f_22 once and `diff` lazily per-symbol.
- Step 5 (genericity): rank is lower-semicontinuous; it can only DROP on special loci. Take the MAX over ≥3 genuinely-octonionic points; a single point can give a false LOW (deficient) rank.

### Approach 2: Fine-grained tier increments (RECOMMENDED ADD-ON — pinpoints the redundant candidate)

**What:** In addition to the full 10-row rank, compute the rank of the first k rows for k = 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 (a fixed candidate ORDER: 6 pointwise, then c, then the 3 mixed in order f_21, f_12, f_22). Each increment that bumps the rank by 1 identifies a functionally-independent addition; an increment that does NOT bump the rank identifies a redundant candidate.

**Why:** The roadmap requires confirming each tier adds independence (6 → 7 → 10). The FINE-GRAINED 6→7→8→9→10 ladder is strictly more informative and is **essential for the fallback**: if the full set stalls at rank 8 or 9, the tier increments tell you EXACTLY which of {f_21, f_12, f_22} is the dependent one, so you know which slot to fill from the fallback ladder. Recommend computing the fine-grained increments unconditionally (cost is negligible — same matrix, prefix ranks).

**Expected (the success path):** ranks (6, 7, 8, 9, 10) — every tier adds 1. This also yields two free independent cross-checks: the **6-pointwise sub-rank = 6** (= 3+3, matches the single-copy trdeg-3 result, Garibaldi-Guralnick / Faraut-Korányi) and the **+c sub-rank = 7** (previews the Phase-66 SPINE: c functionally independent of the pointwise sextet).

**When to switch to the fallback:** if the 10-row MAX rank < 10 (i.e. 8 or 9).

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Float rank on the decisive path (`numpy.linalg.matrix_rank`, `lstsq`, SVD tol):** GLOBAL FORBIDDEN PROXY. Use `exact_qq_rank` (DomainMatrix-over-QQ). numpy is fine ONLY for a non-decisive pre-screen.
- **Assuming the candidate set is complete without computing the Jacobian rank:** forbidden. The deliverable is the COMPUTED rank.
- **Redefining invariants to force the count to 10:** forbidden (reward-hacking guard). If the natural three are deficient, REPORT the deficiency and walk the deterministic fallback ladder to the actual minimal completing set.
- **Non-generic evaluation point** (X=Y, X∝Y, repeated spectra, real-only/diagonal, discriminant locus): gives a false LOW rank. Use the certified generic `PAIR_POINTS` (and `_pair_not_proportional`).
- **`simplify()` on the symbolic 54-var gradient before substitution:** expression swell. Substitute the integer point FIRST.
- **Single-point rank:** a deficient generic point can under-report. Take MAX over ≥3 points.
- **Confusing field completeness with ring generation:** rank 10 proves the candidates generate a subfield of full trdeg (field-complete); it does NOT prove they generate the invariant RING (Phase 68). State the distinction in the plan and the SUMMARY.

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Grouping ambiguity in the non-associative mixed monomials (RESOLVED here)
**What goes wrong:** h_3(O) under `jordan` is power-associative but NON-associative, so `Tr(X²∘Y)` could a priori mean Tr((X∘X)∘Y) or Tr(X∘(X∘Y)), which differ as ALGEBRA elements.
**Why it doesn't bite here:** the TRACE bilinear form is associative — `Tr((A∘B)∘C) = Tr(A∘(B∘C))` — verified in-engine (this RESEARCH) at a generic octonionic point. So `Tr(X²∘Y)` is grouping-independent. Pin the builder as `Tr(jordan(jordan(X,X),Y))` (X² := jordan(X,X) is unambiguous; the outer grouping is irrelevant under Tr).
**Warning sign:** if Tr((X∘X)∘Y) − Tr(X∘(X∘Y)) ≠ 0 at a generic point, the engine `jordan`/`Tr` reuse is wrong — STOP. (It is 0; re-confirm as a module assertion.)
**Recovery:** use the canonical `Tr(jordan(jordan(X,X),Y))` form; assert grouping invariance once at module load.

### Pitfall 2: Expression swell in the deg-3/deg-4 gradients
**What goes wrong:** symbolic `diff` of det X (deg 3) and especially Tr(X²∘Y²) (deg 4) produces large expressions; `simplify()` on the full 54-var gradient explodes.
**Avoidance:** substitute the generic INTEGER point into the partials FIRST (per `_gradients_in_X`), use SMALL integer coordinates (avoid fraction blowup), do NOT `simplify()` the symbolic gradient. Build f_22 once; `diff` per-symbol.

### Pitfall 3: Mistaking field-completeness for ring-generation
**What goes wrong:** reporting "the 10 invariants generate the ring" from rank 10.
**Avoidance:** rank 10 ⇒ field-level functional completeness (the invariant field is algebraic over Q(f_1..f_10)) ONLY. Ring generation (and Krull dimension) is Phase 68. State this boundary in the plan and SUMMARY.

### Pitfall 4: Float rank / non-generic point (FORBIDDEN proxies)
**Avoidance:** `exact_qq_rank` over QQ; generic `PAIR_POINTS`; MAX over ≥3 points; `exact_only_guard`.

### Pitfall 5: Forcing the count (reward-hacking)
**What goes wrong:** if the natural three give rank 9, redefining a candidate or hand-picking a point to reach 10.
**Avoidance:** pre-register the target (10) as a CHECK, not an input; on a deficit, report it and walk the deterministic fallback ladder to the actual minimal completing set. The honest computed minimal set is the deliverable.
