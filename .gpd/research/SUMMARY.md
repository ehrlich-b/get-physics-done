## Two Reconciliations the Scouts Surfaced (resolved here, not papered over)

### Reconciliation 1 — POLARIZATION: does polarizing {Tr, Tr², det} generate the pair ring? **NO, do not assume it.**

**The conflict.** METHODS.md leans **YES** ("polarizing a single-copy generating set DOES yield a generating set for any number of copies"), invoking the char-0 Weyl polarization theorem with no exceptional-group caveat. PITFALLS.md **CORRECTS** this: two different theorems travel under "polarization," and METHODS.md conflates them.

**Diagnosis (not a numerical disagreement — a precise theorem mix-up).**
- **Weyl's polarization theorem** (Domokos–Kemper Thm 1.3, char 0): if S generates K[V^n]^G for **n = dim V = 27 copies**, then polarizations of S generate K[V^m]^G for all m ≥ n. This is true for all reductive G including F_4 — but it starts from **27 copies**, not 1.
- **The "2-polarization property"** (Schwarz, arXiv:math/0609078): polarizing the **single-copy** generators of C[V]^G generates the 2-copy ring. Schwarz's main theorem *classifies* which (G, V) have this property, and **"most representations do NOT have the 2-polarization property — even over C, char 0."**

The trap is to read "Weyl polarization holds in char 0" as "polarizing 1 copy generates," which is the 2-polarization property, which is generically false.

**Resolution (HIGH confidence, independently verified).** Do **NOT** assume polarization gives a complete generating set for the F_4 pair. Treat the polarized det-cross-terms {f(X,X,Y), f(X,Y,Y)} + the trace monomials {Tr(X∘Y), and possibly Tr(X²∘Y), Tr(X∘Y²), Tr(X²∘Y²)} as **CANDIDATE** generators, and **CERTIFY completeness via the bigraded Hilbert/Molien series** (degree-by-degree match to total degree ≤ 6). This is exactly what PRIOR-WORK recommends ("instantiate + prune + verify"; Blind 2011 E_6 result + Iltyakov F_4 trace polynomials). The char-0 Weyl theorem is *not* an obstacle and *not* a free pass — it bounds nothing useful here from a single copy; the Hilbert-series certificate is the actual content of (a). [Independently verified: Schwarz arXiv:math/0609078 — 2-polarization fails for D_4 Weyl group and "most" representations.]

**Roadmap impact:** (a) is a *certification* phase, not a *derivation* phase, and its primary risk/deliverable is the Hilbert-series match. A mismatch at any bidegree (d_true > d_candidate) means a genuinely-new generator is missing there.

## Executive Summary

The (RING) lemma asks for the structure of the joint F_4-invariant ring R[h_3(O) ⊕ h_3(O)]^{F_4}, with three sub-claims: (a) an explicit generating set, (b) functional independence of the coupling c = Tr(X∘Y) from the six single-copy ("pointwise") generators, and (c) uniqueness of c as the degree-2 coupling generator modulo products. The single-copy ring R[h_3(O)]^{F_4} = R[Tr, Tr², det] (3 algebraically independent generators) is established and must not be re-derived. The current state of the literature is that the F_4 pair ring is **"morally solved but not packaged"**: Blind (2011) proves the *E_6* (det-stabilizer) pair ring is a free algebra on 4 det-polarizations, and Iltyakov (1998) shows F_4 several-copy invariants are generated by trace polynomials + Laplace operators with the rational-invariant field equal to Frac(trace algebra) for ≤ 2 copies — but no source writes out a minimal F_4 two-copy generating set with proven independence/uniqueness. The milestone is therefore **verification, completion, and citation**, executed as exact-SymPy computation over Q.

The recommended path is "instantiate + prune + verify." Build the candidate generators (six pointwise + c + the polarized mixed cubics), then certify them with exact-arithmetic tools: a Jacobian-rank computation for (b), a Sym² branching count for (c), and a bigraded Hilbert/Molien-series match for (a). The proof spine is **(b)**: c is the trace-form coupling, it is F_4-invariant but not E_6-invariant, and "the complete third-person (single-frame) record does not determine Φ" is precisely the statement c ∉ R_pt. This must be **demonstrated** — a rank-7 Jacobian over Q at ≥3 generic rational points AND a nonzero infinitesimal orbit-derivative of c (while every pointwise generator has zero derivative). Both routes are mandatory under the reward-hacking guard, and **a rank-6 outcome (c IS in the pointwise ring) is a decisive, fully acceptable NEGATIVE** that must be reported with a constructed explicit expression, not buried.

The two principal risks are now resolved (see Reconciliations): (1) **do not assume polarization generates** the pair ring — the single-copy "2-polarization property" generically fails even in char 0 (Schwarz), so (a) is a Hilbert-series certification obligation, not a polarization hand-wave; (2) **run the decisive ranks on the exact-SymPy engine** (`embedding_under_E_verification.py`), not the float64 `octonion_algebra.py`, because float rank fabricates the 6-vs-7 verdict. The highest-leverage single computation, gating everything, is the **exact generic orbit dimension of F_4 on 27⊕27** (rank of the 52×54 infinitesimal-action matrix over Q); the naive Spin(8)-triality count is a trap and must be computed, with the consistency anchor 54 − orbit_dim = 7. (REDUCIBILITY) is to be **STATED only** — never assert open-system irreducibility, never use a chaos/NKS argument, and flag the autonomous-vs-driven trap.

### Critical Pitfalls

1. **[(a)] Assuming polarization generates** — the single-copy 2-polarization property fails generically even in char 0 (Schwarz). Avoid by the Hilbert-series completeness certificate (Reconciliation 1).
2. **[(b)] Float Jacobian rank** — rank is discontinuous; an SVD tolerance fabricates the 6-vs-7 verdict. Use exact `Matrix.rank()` over Q on the exact engine (Reconciliation 2).
3. **[(b)] Non-generic evaluation point** — special points (X=Y, repeated spectra, X ∝ Y, discriminant locus) drop the rank. Use ≥3 generic rational points (all-nonzero, distinct diagonals, X ≠ λY); X=Y must correctly give ≤6 and is *not* an independence test point.
4. **[gate] Wrong orbit dimension / Spin(8)-triality back-of-envelope** — corrupts every transcendence-degree statement (the naive count gives orbit 52 ⇒ trdeg 2, contradicting the 7 known invariants). Compute the infinitesimal-action rank; cross-check 54 − orbit_dim = Krull dim.
5. **[(b)] Forcing a positive over the honest NEGATIVE** — a rank-6 result kills the mechanism and is a full pass; pre-register the exact test, and if rank 6, construct and report the explicit P with c = P(g_1..g_6).
6. **[(c)] Dropping the "mod products" qualifier / 26-vs-27 confusion** — there are TWO bidegree-(1,1) invariants {Tr(X)Tr(Y), c}; c is unique only modulo the reducible product and pointwise terms. Carry 27 = 1 ⊕ 26 explicitly.

## Theoretical Connections

- **E_6 ↔ F_4 (ESTABLISHED).** Both stabilize structure on the 27: E_6 = Stab(det), F_4 = Aut = Stab(det, Tr, trace form). The pair rings differ precisely by the trace-form coupling: R[2·27]^{F_4} ⊋ C[2·27]^{E_6}|_R, the extra generator being c = Tr(X∘Y). Blind's clean 4-generator E_6 answer is the *contrast/lower bound*, and the gap between the two rings is the mathematical home of the Chalmers cross-term. This is the single most important structural connection.
- **27 = 1 ⊕ 26 under F_4 (ESTABLISHED).** Drives every branching count: the "1" (Tr direction) gives the reducible product Tr(X)Tr(Y); the "26" (trace-free) gives the genuine coupling via 26⊗26 → 1. dim End_{F_4}(1⊕26) = 2 is exactly why there are two (1,1)-invariants.
- **F_4 ⊃ Spin(8) by triality (ESTABLISHED, but a trap source).** Single-copy generic stabilizer = Spin(8); the *pair* stabilizer is NOT obtained by naive triality (the three 8's are permuted; a generic element of one is not trivially stabilized). The pair principal isotropy must be computed.
- **Jacobian criterion ↔ orbit geometry (ESTABLISHED).** trdeg = generic Jacobian rank = dim of the function field = 54 − (generic orbit dim). The (b) Jacobian rank and the orbit-derivative argument are two faces of the same fact — which is why the milestone demands both as independent confirmations.
- **Plethystics bridge (CONJECTURED-by-analogy, useful).** The Hilbert/Molien-series + plethystic-log workflow is identical to physics gauge-rep invariant-ring computations (Hanany et al., "Standard Model Plethystics," arXiv:1902.10550) — the same machinery reads off generators and syzygies.
